# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want `relaybox` to be a respectful, welcoming project for contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

- be respectful and constructive
- assume good intent
- give actionable technical feedback
- prioritize clarity over status

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment or discrimination
- insulting or demeaning language
- deliberate disruption of discussions or reviews

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate this code of conduct.
